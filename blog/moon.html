Personal Reflections

10. moon

March 25, 20264 min read

she goes by a few names.

umeeh. ummi. moon.

there is a specific kind of quiet that settles over me when we talk, the kind that i don't really get anywhere else. when everything else is loud — when blue particle studios is hectic, when code campus is demanding, when hackathons are burning through my brain cells — she is just steady.

it's rare to find someone who doesn't demand a performance from you. someone who is perfectly fine with the silent, completely exhausted version of you that shows up at the end of a fourteen-hour day of building things. she doesn't need me to be the team lead or the founder or the guy who has everything figured out. she just lets me exist in the space next to her.

i don't talk about it much on here because some things are better kept away from the screen. some things lose their texture when you try to turn them into paragraphs for public consumption. but i just wanted to write it down so it exists somewhere permanent. a small note in the ledger.

she's my moon, and the sky is a lot less empty when she's around.
